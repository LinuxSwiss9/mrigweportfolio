About

Editing with intention,
not decoration.

I'm a video editor based in Nigeria, working with creators, businesses and production teams who need their footage turned into clear, engaging videos.

My approach is simple: understand what the video needs to achieve and who it's for, then shape every cut, sound and frame around that goal. Whether it's a talking-head video that needs tighter delivery, a documentary that needs structure, or a promo that needs to land in seconds, I focus on pacing, story and polish.

I communicate clearly, take feedback seriously and respect deadlines — so working together is straightforward from the first brief to the final export.

Work process

From footage to final cut

A clear, simple process so you always know where your project stands.

  1. Brief

    We discuss your goals, audience, platform, style references, length and deadline.

  2. Footage & assembly

    I review and organise your footage, then build the structure of the story.

  3. First cut

    You receive a first version to review and share clear, timestamped feedback.

  4. Refine & polish

    Revisions, colour, sound, captions and graphics are refined until it's right.

  5. Delivery

    Final export in the formats and aspect ratios you need, ready to publish.
